Privacy Policy

Summary: Reki: Http Client is designed to help you create and send HTTP requests. We do not sell your personal data or use it for behavioural advertising.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Reki: Http Client (“Reki”, “the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the App. It applies to the mobile application and related features we provide.

2. Data the App May Process

To provide its HTTP client functionality, Reki temporarily processes data that you enter into the App for the active request, including URLs, HTTP methods, headers, query parameters, cookies, request bodies, response bodies, and credentials such as API keys or bearer tokens.

Reki uses this data solely to send the request you initiate and display the resulting response. Reki does not save, retain, create a history of, or upload your request data, response data, credentials, or request configuration. Do not include sensitive information in a request unless it is necessary for your purpose and you trust the destination server.

3. Storage and Data Transmission

Reki does not store requests, responses, credentials, tokens, request history, collections, or environment variables on your device or on servers operated by us. When you choose to send a request, the App transmits the data in that request directly to the server or service that you specify. The destination server’s processing of data is governed by its own privacy policy and terms, not this Privacy Policy.

We do not upload, synchronize, or retain your request data, response data, tokens, or request history.

4. Data We Do Not Collect

Reki does not require an account and does not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, audio, messages, or health information. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Reki does not use advertising SDKs, does not display personalized advertisements, and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. The App does not use your data to identify or track you.

6. Your Choices and Controls

You can view, edit, or delete your requests, collections, history, and settings within the App. You can also remove local App data using your device’s system controls, such as “Clear app data”, or by uninstalling the App. Removing the App may not delete data already sent to a destination server; contact that server’s operator if needed.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of data stored by the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device and safeguarding credentials you enter into Reki.

8. Device Permissions

Reki requests only permissions needed to provide the features you use. If a system permission is requested in a future version, the App will explain its purpose at the time of the request. You can manage or revoke permissions in your device settings.

9. Children’s Privacy

Reki is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can review and address the matter.

10. International Data Transfers

Because Reki sends requests to servers you choose, data contained in a request may be transmitted to or processed in another country, depending on the location and configuration of the destination server. You choose the destination server when using the request-sending functionality.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App or applicable legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new “Effective date”. Where required, we will provide appropriate notice of material changes in the App or through the app store listing.
