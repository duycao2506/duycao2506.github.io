Your privacy, simply statedHow strong you can shake ? does not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell your personal information.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of How strong you can shake ? (the “Game”) handles information when you use the Game. The Game measures how many shakes you can perform during a set period of time.

2. Information and device sensors

The Game uses your device’s motion sensors, such as the accelerometer, only while you play to detect shakes and show your result. Motion-sensor readings are processed on your device and are not collected, stored, or transmitted by us.

The Game does not collect personal information, including your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, IP address, usage data, diagnostic data, crash reports, purchase information, or advertising identifiers.

The Game does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, cloud storage, leaderboards, or third-party data-collection services.

3. How information is used

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not use personal information for any purpose. Motion-sensor readings are used only on your device to provide the shake-counting gameplay feature.

4. Sharing and sale of information

We do not share, sell, rent, disclose, or otherwise provide personal information to third parties. We do not use personal information for targeted advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Data retention and deletion

We do not retain personal information because we do not collect it. Motion-sensor readings used during gameplay remain on your device only for the time needed to count shakes and display the result.

6. Permissions and controls

The Game may access motion-sensor functionality on your device solely to detect shakes. You can manage device permissions through your device settings. Disabling any required motion access may prevent the shake-counting feature from working correctly.

7. Children’s privacy

The Game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe that information has been sent to us through another channel, please contact us at the email address below.

8. Security and international transfers

Because we do not collect or transmit personal information through the Game, we do not maintain personal-information databases or make international transfers of personal information through the Game.

9. Your privacy rights

Since the Game does not collect or retain personal information, there is no personal information held by us for you to access, correct, delete, or transfer. If you have a privacy question, you may contact us using the email address below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game or its privacy practices change. We will update the effective date at the top of this page when we do.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions, contact us at duydaodac@gmail.com.
