Privacy Policy

Chain Math: Neon Numbers (the "Game") · Developer: Duy Cao
Effective date: November 18, 2026

Summary: Chain Math works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no servers of our own, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your game progress, coins, and purchases are stored locally and encrypted. The only data that leaves your device is what Apple, Google, and (for purchase verification) RevenueCat process when you make an in-app purchase, or what Apple Game Center / Google Play Games Services process if you choose to sign in to an optional leaderboard.

1. Who we are

This policy is provided by Duy Cao, the developer of Chain Math: Neon Numbers. Contact: duydaodac@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or contacts, and does not send your gameplay data to us or to anyone else.

Everything the Game stores stays on your device:

WhatWhere it is storedWhy
Game progress: scores, records, statistics, and active-run state On your device, in an encrypted local store So you can keep playing where you left off and see your records
Coin ledger (earned and purchased coins) and item inventory On your device, in an encrypted local store (encryption keys held in the iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) To track your in-game currency and power-ups
Settings (sound, haptics, theme) On your device, in standard local preferences To remember your choices
Pending leaderboard submissions (your endless-mode high score) On your device, in an encrypted local store, until delivered or cleared To submit your score if and only if you signed in to the optional platform leaderboard

The Game does not use device permissions such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

3. In-app purchases

Chain Math sells consumable coin packs. Purchases are processed by Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android), which handle your payment information under their own privacy policies. We never see or receive your full payment details.

To verify purchases and recover coins from transactions that were interrupted before being credited, the Game uses the RevenueCat SDK. RevenueCat processes purchase history and device identifiers for purchase verification and app functionality, not for advertising or cross-app tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Coins are stored on this device and cannot be transferred to another device. They are not guaranteed to be recoverable after app deletion, reinstallation, or a device reset.

4. Optional platform leaderboards

If you choose to connect to an endless-mode leaderboard, the Game uses Apple Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games Services (Android). When connected, your high score and your platform game profile are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy terms:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Leaderboards are entirely optional. If you never sign in, the Game works fully offline and nothing is submitted anywhere. Your coin balance, purchase history, and inventory are never shared with Game Center or Play Games Services — only an endless-mode high score is submitted, and only after it has been saved locally.

5. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The Game contains:

  • no analytics or telemetry,
  • no advertising or ad SDKs,
  • no cross-app or cross-site tracking,
  • no third-party SDKs other than RevenueCat (purchase verification) and, only if you opt in, the Apple / Google game services described above.

Fonts used by the Game are bundled with the app; the Game does not fetch fonts or other assets from the network at runtime. Apart from purchase verification and optional leaderboard submission, the Game makes no network requests.

This privacy policy page itself loads no external scripts, sets no cookies, and uses no analytics.
