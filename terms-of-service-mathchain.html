7. Power-ups (virtual items)

Coins buy three in-game power-ups. Each purchase is a virtual item consumed on use:

ItemEffectCoin cost
Continue After time expires, keep the same grid, target, and partial chain and add 10 seconds. Once per round. 75
+Time Add 15 seconds immediately; round time is capped at 60 seconds. 40
Skip Replace the current round; gain 0 points and reset the streak. 30

Each device-local profile receives one free starter use of each item, granted once ever. After that, items are available only by purchase with coins and are capped at three of each type.
